The Crohn's Veteran Podcast

From April 2020 to World IBD Day 2023, Jordan and co-hosts CJ “Papuro” Cabrera and Renika Wood spoke with people living with Crohn's and colitis, and with the doctors, caregivers, authors, and advocates beside them. The show no longer releases new episodes. The full archive is free to stream.
