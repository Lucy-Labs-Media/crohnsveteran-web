Questions

Before you buy

How does checkout work?

Choose an item here, then click “Choose size & buy.” You will land on that product's page at our Printful store, where you pick the size and color and complete your order.

Are the sizes and colors the same on every item?

No. Each item lists its own options on the Printful product page. Check the size chart there before ordering.

I have a question about an order.

Include your order number and email jordan@crohnsveteran.com. For order status, shipping, or returns, Printful's order support handles the fulfillment directly.

Can I buy for a child or a teen?

Yes. The You're Awesome! youth cap is sized for kids and teens. The other items are adult sizes. Men's and women's slides are sized for their own fits, so check the size chart on each product page.
